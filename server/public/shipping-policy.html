Shipping & Service Fulfillment Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 17, 2026 • Entity: QrPrintDesk Technologies
Key Summary: QrPrintDesk provides software-enabled digital queueing and contactless printing. Service fulfillment occurs in two seamless stages: (1) Instant digital order token generation upon successful payment (1–3 seconds), and (2) On-counter physical handover of printed documents at the selected store counter. There are no shipping or courier charges.

1. Nature of Products & Services

QrPrintDesk Technologies operates a software platform connecting walk-in customers with retail photocopy and print center counters. We deliver digital queueing infrastructure, automated file spooling, and merchant point-of-sale software.

2. Customer Print Orders: Fulfillment Model

A. Stage 1: Instant Electronic Delivery (Digital Token)

Upon successful payment verification via our authorized payment gateway, delivery of your digital order token and receipt occurs instantaneously (within 1 to 3 seconds) directly inside your mobile browser window. A unique Order Token Number (e.g. #7482) is displayed on your screen.

B. Stage 2: Physical Counter Pickup & Handover

The physical printing of your document begins immediately in the store's FIFO (first-in, first-out) queue.

  • Fulfillment Location: The physical photocopy / print store where you scanned the counter QR code.
  • Turnaround Time (TAT): Typically 1 to 5 minutes from payment confirmation, depending on document length and queue volume.
  • Proof of Delivery: You receive your physical printed documents directly from the store operator by showing your Order Token Number on your mobile phone.

3. Shipping / Courier Charges

Because printouts are fulfilled and collected directly by the customer in person at the local store counter, no shipping, postage, or courier charges apply to customer print transactions. The price quoted on the mobile checkout screen is the all-inclusive total price.

4. Vendor Software Delivery (For Print Shop Owners)

For photocopy shop owners subscribing to QrPrintDesk software:

  • Delivery Mode: 100% Digital Download.
  • Access: The Windows Vendor Station software installer (`QrPrintDesk_Vendor_Setup.exe`) and web portal access credentials are delivered instantly online immediately after registration.
  • Installation Support: Remote setup guidance and digital onboarding materials are provided at zero additional cost.

5. Non-Collection / Unclaimed Physical Prints

Customers are advised to collect their physical printed pages at the counter promptly. If an order is printed but left unclaimed at the retail shop, the physical paper prints will be held securely by the store operator until the close of business hours on that calendar day.

6. Assistance & Fulfillment Inquiries

If you experience any delay in receiving your digital token or collecting your printed order at the counter, please speak directly to the store operator or contact our centralized support team:
