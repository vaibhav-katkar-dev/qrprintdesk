Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 17, 2026 • Entity: QrPrintDesk Technologies
Key Summary: QrPrintDesk is an automated cloud print queue and shop management platform. Customer files uploaded for printing are strictly ephemeral: they are encrypted in transit, spooled to the store printer for execution, and permanently wiped from cloud storage within 24 hours. We do not sell or monetize personal data.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to QrPrintDesk ("we", "our", "us", or "Platform"), operated by QrPrintDesk Technologies. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and safeguard data when customers ("Users") upload documents to print at participating photocopy/print stores, and when store owners ("Vendors" or "Merchants") utilize our desktop software and cloud services.

This policy is formulated in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP).

2. Information We Collect

A. Customer / User Data (End Users Printing Documents)

  • Print Job Files: Digital files (PDFs, images, identity card scans) uploaded voluntarily by the user solely to execute the requested print job.
  • Print Configuration: Page selection, color mode (Black & White vs Color), duplex settings, and number of copies.
  • Transaction Identifiers: Digital token numbers and payment reference IDs generated by authorized payment gateways. We do not store customer credit card numbers, debit card numbers, or UPI PINs.
  • Contact Details (Optional): Phone number or email address only if entered by the user to receive digital receipts or order status SMS.

B. Vendor / Merchant Data (Store Owners)

  • Account & Store Information: Store name, proprietor name, business email address, contact telephone number, and physical shop address.
  • Settlement & Payout Details: Bank account number, IFSC code, and PAN or GSTIN as required by Indian banking regulations and payment aggregators for daily automated settlement.
  • Hardware Information: Printer make/model and Windows device OS version necessary for print spooler driver synchronization.

3. Ephemeral Document Lifecycle & Deletion Policy

The confidentiality of customer documents is paramount. QrPrintDesk enforces a strict ephemeral lifecycle:

  1. Encryption in Transit: Files are transmitted over 256-bit TLS/SSL encrypted channels directly to isolated, signed storage buckets.
  2. Execution: The vendor's local desktop station receives the file via encrypted websocket/tunnel and delivers it directly to the Windows Print Spooler.
  3. Automated Purge: Once the print job is completed or cancelled, or after a maximum retention window of 24 hours, the uploaded file is permanently and irreversibly purged from our cloud servers and object storage.

4. Use of Information

We process collected data solely for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Facilitating the transmission, queueing, and printing of documents requested by the user.
  • Computing accurate upfront billing based on the merchant's configured page tariff.
  • Processing digital payments and routing settlements via RBI-regulated payment partners.
  • Providing technical assistance, preventing unauthorized usage, and complying with statutory obligations.

5. Payment Processing & Banking Compliance

Online payments made through QrPrintDesk (including UPI, Net Banking, and Wallets) are processed through Reserve Bank of India (RBI) authorized payment aggregators (such as Cashfree Payments). All sensitive payment handling complies with Payment Card Industry Data Security Standards (PCI-DSS Level 1). QrPrintDesk does not store sensitive cardholder data, CVVs, or bank credentials.

6. Data Security Practices

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational measures to safeguard data against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. These include end-to-end HTTPS/TLS encryption, time-limited presigned URLs, role-based access control, firewall protections, and regular vulnerability audits.

7. Disclosure of Information

QrPrintDesk does not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties. Information may only be disclosed in the following narrow circumstances:

  • Authorized Infrastructure Providers: Cloud hosting, encrypted storage, and payment gateway partners bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If mandated by law, court order, or lawful government agency request under applicable Indian statutes.

8. Your Rights Under Indian Law

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request rectification of inaccurate data, and request erasure of your account or personal details. Since customer print files are auto-purged within 24 hours, historical file data is not stored long-term.
