Why Traditional Xerox Counters Lose Money Every Day

The Old Counter Struggles

  • ✕ USB Pendrive Viruses: Infected customer thumb drives corrupting shop PCs, crashing Windows spoolers, and wiping business records.
  • ✕ WhatsApp Clutter & Leaks: Customers spamming your personal WhatsApp number with 50MB files, mixing business files with private family chats.
  • ✕ Page Count Disputes: Arguments over whether a 40-page PDF was printed single-sided or double-sided, or if colors were accidental.
  • ✕ Counter Choke: Crowds of students waiting impatiently around one computer screen while the operator manually opens email after email.

The QrPrintDesk Automation

  • ✓ Zero Hardware Touch: Customers scan your counter QR standee using their own smartphones. No cables, no pendrives, no virus risk.
  • ✓ No App Download Required: Runs instantly inside Chrome, Safari, or PayTM/GPay scanner in under 1 second as a lightweight web app.
  • ✓ Transparent Upfront Billing: Automatic page counts, duplex discounts, and color mode calculations displayed before payment.
  • ✓ Automated Silent Spooling: Paid jobs spool directly to the printer in a clean FIFO queue. The shopkeeper simply hands over the prints.
