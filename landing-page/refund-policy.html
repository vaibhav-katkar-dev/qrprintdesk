Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 17, 2026 • Entity: QrPrintDesk Technologies
Summary: If your print job cannot be completed due to technical errors, store printer malfunction, or multiple deductions, you are 100% entitled to a full refund. Approved refunds are credited directly back to your original payment method (UPI / Bank Account) within 5 to 7 business days.

1. Overview

At QrPrintDesk Technologies ("QrPrintDesk"), customer satisfaction and billing transparency are our highest priorities. Because our service facilitates physical printout generation on demand, this policy sets out the exact conditions under which cancellations and refunds are granted.

2. Customer Print Orders: Eligible Refund Scenarios

Customers are eligible for an immediate full refund under the following circumstances:

  • Failed Transaction / Technical Error: Money was deducted from your UPI app, bank account, or card, but the platform failed to generate a print job token or transmit the order to the store.
  • Duplicate / Multiple Deductions: Accidental double payment for the same single print job. All duplicate excess debits will be refunded automatically.
  • Store Hardware Malfunction: The store printer encounters an unrecoverable failure (e.g. fatal paper jam, hardware breakdown, out of paper/toner) and the merchant is unable to fulfill the job.
  • Store Rejection: The merchant declines the print order (e.g., store closing hours, inappropriate file content) before printing begins.

3. Cancellation Policy

A. Pre-Execution Cancellation

You may request cancellation of an order at the counter with the shop operator as long as the status is marked as "Queued" or "Pending" and the physical printer has not yet begun outputting sheets.

B. Post-Execution (Non-Cancellable)

Once a print job has been spooled and physical printing has completed onto paper, the order cannot be cancelled, as physical materials (toner, paper sheets) have already been permanently consumed. Please double-check your page range, color mode, and copy count on mobile before making payment.

4. Defective or Incorrect Prints

If a printout is visibly defective due to mechanical printer errors (e.g., severe toner streaks, ink smearing, truncated pages caused by printer malfunction), please immediately alert the store operator at the counter:

  • Immediate Counter Reprint: In most cases, the store operator will reprint your job immediately at zero extra charge.
  • Refund Request: If the merchant cannot reprint the order, the merchant or our support desk will process an electronic refund for the defective portion.

5. Refund Turnaround Time (TAT) & Payout Process

6. How to Request a Refund

If you encounter any billing discrepancy, raising a refund request is straightforward:

  1. Option 1 (Fastest): Notify the shopkeeper at the counter immediately with your 4-digit Token ID. The vendor can trigger a one-tap cancellation on their Vendor Station.
  2. Option 2 (Online Support Desk): Email our dedicated support team at refunds@qrprintdesk.in or support@qrprintdesk.in with:
    • Your Order Token ID or Shop Name.
    • Transaction Reference / UTR Number from your UPI or banking app.
    • Date and approximate time of the transaction.
    • Brief description of the issue (e.g. money debited but token failed).

Our payment reconciliation team will investigate and process eligible refunds within 24 business hours.

7. Merchant Subscription Refund Policy

For store owners subscribing to paid QrPrintDesk software tiers:

  • 7-Day Trial / Money-Back Guarantee: First-time merchant subscribers who are dissatisfied with the software may request a 100% full refund within 7 days of initial subscription activation.
  • Monthly Subscriptions: Ongoing monthly subscriptions may be cancelled anytime with zero penalty. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle.
